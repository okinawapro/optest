The saveTALK Framework

In high-stress environments, peer awareness saves lives. The saveTALK methodology breaks suicide alertness down into four distinct steps:

1. Talk (Identify the signs)

Do not ignore invitations to help. When someone indicates distress physically, verbally, or behaviorally, pay close attention. Act immediately instead of assuming someone else will.

2. Ask (The direct approach)

  • Never beat around the bush or use vague euphemisms like "are you planning to do something silly?".
  • Ask clearly and directly: "Are you thinking about suicide?". This shows you are ready to talk about the reality of what they are experiencing.

3. Listen (Give full attention)

  • Avoid giving immediate solutions, dismissing their pain, or telling them to "tough it out."
  • Listen actively without judgment. Allow them to express their feelings. Their pain is real to them.

4. KeepSafe (Connect to help)

Never agree to keep suicidal thoughts a secret. Connect them directly to a trained caregiver, a suicide safety resource, or the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Walk with them through this process; do not just hand over a phone number and walk away.
